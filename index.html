Features

Everything for planning a circuit in one place

From the first sketch to a checked design, Wirebench keeps the drawing, the rules and the reference material together.

Component library

Mains supply, breakers, fuses, RCDs, switches, lights, sockets, appliances, junction boxes and neutral and earth bars.

Interactive wiring

Click one terminal, then another. Wire type is chosen automatically from the terminals, with cable size set per run.

Automatic safety checks

Flags unprotected loads, missing RCDs, open neutrals and earths, wrong wire colours, and cable that is too small for its breaker.

Project management

Save, duplicate and organise projects with thumbnails and status badges. Export and import any project as JSON.

Personal dashboard

See open issues across every project, jump straight to the part that needs fixing, and track design scores over time.

Guides and calculators

Wire colours, cable and breaker sizing, earthing, a safe-working checklist and a voltage-drop calculator, beside your diagram.

How it works

From blank canvas to checked circuit in three steps

Start from a template or an empty grid. The checks update after every change.

STEP 1

Place components

Pick parts from the palette and drop them on the grid. Rotate, label and set ratings in the inspector.

STEP 2

Connect the wires

Join terminals with live, neutral, earth and switched-live conductors, and choose a cable size for each run.

STEP 3

Review the checks

Fix anything flagged, read the matching guide, then save the project to your dashboard.

Safety checks

Catch common mistakes before they reach the wall

Wirebench traces every circuit back to the supply and checks the details that are easy to miss on paper.

  • Every load has a breaker or fuse in its supply path
  • Sockets are behind an RCD
  • Neutral and earth reach the supply
  • Wire type matches each terminal
  • Cable size suits the breaker rating
  • Connected load stays within the breaker rating

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Wirebench free to use?

There are no plans or paywalls in this version. Create an account or continue as a guest and use every feature.

Do I need an account?

No. As a guest you can use everything straight away. An account with a username and password keeps your profile and projects separate from anyone else using the same browser. Accounts are stored on this device only, so they do not follow you to another browser or device.

Does Wirebench replace an electrician?

No. The checks catch common drawing mistakes. They are a planning aid and do not replace a qualified electrician or your local wiring rules, and many places require fixed wiring to be done or certified by a licensed professional.

Which supply voltages and regions are supported?

You choose a region and default supply voltage in your profile, and each project can use 120 V, 230 V or 240 V. Wire colour references cover UK and EU and US and Canada conventions.

Can I take my diagrams with me?

Yes. Any project can be exported as JSON from the Projects page and imported again later or on another device.
